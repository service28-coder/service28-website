How a night goes

The run of service, called from the pass.

  1. 7:00 PM

    Doors

    Welcome drink. Shoes off. Find your seat at the long table.

  2. Course one

    Fire

    Eight or nine courses, one after another, sent out together.

  3. Somewhere mid-menu

    Pass

    The bottles you brought start going round the table.

  4. 10:00 PM

    Last plate

    Something sweet, then the kitchen exhales.

Sometimes we'll make something strange. Sometimes we'll keep it simple. Sometimes we'll completely change our minds.

What doesn't change: we start on time, and the menu you RSVP'd for is the menu you get. You should leave feeling like you were part of something, not just that you had dinner.

House rules

One menu, no swaps

Everyone eats the same set menu. Each dinner lists what it contains. If you can't eat something on that list, skip this one and catch the next.

BYOB, no corkage

Bring whatever you like to drink. Glasses are on us.

If plans change

  1. 7+ days out

    Full refund

  2. 72 hours to 7 days

    Credit for a future dinner

  3. Under 72 hours or no-show

    No refund, but pass your seat to a friend

  4. If we cancel

    Full refund, always

From inquiry to service

How it works

  1. 01 · today

    Send an inquiry

    Date, guests, format, occasion, where. The form below takes a minute.

  2. 02 · within 24 to 36 hours

    We talk it through

    We reply, ask about the people and the mood, and what to avoid.

  3. 03 · within 3 to 5 days

    Get your proposal

    A written menu and a clear price for your table.

  4. 04 · deposit paid

    Lock the date

    A 50% deposit holds it. Then we start cooking in our heads.

Read before you book

The fine print

  1. More than 30 days out

    Deposit back, minus venue fees already paid

  2. 8 to 30 days out

    Deposit kept, move the date once within 3 months

  3. 7 days or less

    No refund

  4. If we cancel

    Full refund, always

Booking under 30 days out? The deposit is non-refundable from the day you pay it. You can still move the date once, up to 8 days before.

Mise en place

An evening, not just dinner.

A restaurant is somewhere you go to eat. An evening is somewhere you go to be part of something.

Service 28 is MJ and Selina. We care about the food, obviously. We also care about everything around it: who you're sitting with, what you're listening to, what's in your glass, and how the night moves.
